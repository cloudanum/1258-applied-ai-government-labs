# Do Now 9.D: Who Do You Need? (Optional) 🎯

**Time:** 5 minutes
**Format:** No sandbox or tooling required, this activity is done mentally and captured directly on the Mural board (or in Zoom chat if you are remote).
**Mural:** https://app.mural.co/t/day10626/m/day10626/1790240819050/8ad233c17ae963c61809bfd015485d96afb17718

## Goal
Map the people your first use case needs before it can launch: sponsor, data owner, security, legal/privacy, operations, and end user. You place the role stickies, mark the one role you do not currently have, and write the first specific ask you would make of that person.

**Assets:** Role stickies, sponsor, data, security, legal/privacy, ops, end user (see `board-items.tsv`).

## Run steps 🪜
1. Open the Mural board linked above and find the role stickies: sponsor, data, security, legal/privacy, ops, end user.
2. Keep your first use case from Do Now 9.B in mind: who must be involved before a pilot could actually start?
3. Drag each role sticky into your use-case area to mark it as needed; working remotely? List the roles in Zoom chat instead.
4. Color-tag or circle the one role you do NOT currently have access to; for many government teams that is privacy/legal.
5. Add a Mural comment with your "first ask" for that missing role, one specific question, e.g. "Can resident complaint text be used in a pilot, and what must be masked?"
6. Compare with a neighbor: did they mark a different role as missing, and why? Remote fallback: post `missing role + first ask` as one line in Zoom chat.

## Key takeaway 💡
AI projects fail on missing people more often than missing technology, naming the one role you lack, and your first specific ask of them, turns "we should really talk to legal" into an actionable step.

## Study further 📚
- [AI adoption planning, Microsoft Cloud Adoption Framework](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/scenarios/ai/plan), official guidance on the skills and roles needed to plan an AI project.
- [NIST AI RMF Playbook, NIST AI Resource Center](https://airc.nist.gov/AI_RMF_Knowledge_Base/Playbook), the Govern function's playbook for assigning AI roles and responsibilities.
- [What is MLOps?, IBM Think](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/mlops), how data, security, operations, and business roles fit together to run AI in production.

## Common mistake to name ⚠️
Placing roles by org-chart habit without saying why; require one placement to be defended out loud, especially the role marked missing, before moving on.

## If finished early ⏩
Add one edge case that would change your map, for example, the use case touches resident PII, and explain in one Mural comment which new role that pulls in.
