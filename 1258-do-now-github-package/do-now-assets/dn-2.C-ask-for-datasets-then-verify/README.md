# Do Now 2.C: Ask for Datasets, Then Verify 🎯

**Time:** 5 minutes
**Format:** No sandbox or special tooling required, this activity can be done mentally (reasoning through what you would ask and how you would check it) and is captured in Zoom chat (in-room participants may add their result to the Mural board instead).
**Mural:** https://app.mural.co/t/day10626/m/day10626/1790240789049/0ff478b72a7508905c90a132dde3e48c04d101d6

## Goal
By the end of this five-minute exercise you will have used an approved AI assistant to suggest two real public datasets on a topic your agency cares about, then personally verified that at least one of them actually exists by opening the URL and confirming it lands on a live, current dataset page. The artifact you produce is a single verified link posted in the capture format `VERIFIED: <url>`, not a list of AI suggestions. The underlying habit is trust-but-verify: AI assistants are excellent discovery engines, but they routinely invent plausible-looking URLs, so a recommendation only becomes useful once a human has opened it. You will leave with one dataset link you can defend, and the instinct to never forward an AI-sourced reference you have not opened yourself.

## Why it matters
Asking AI to find datasets and then verifying they exist is a core AI-at-work habit, verification is the step that separates a useful lead from a hallucinated citation. Doing it in chat makes the reasoning visible and easy to correct, so the whole room learns what a checked link looks like.

**Assets:** Approved AI assistant + web (see `board-items.tsv` for the starter and capture template).

**🌐 MS Co-pilot Specific Info:** Copilot will happily suggest dataset names and URLs, that is exactly the trap this exercise trains: never repost a link you have not opened yourself in the same browser.

## Run steps 🪜
1. Pick a real question your agency works on, flooding, wait times, air quality, service requests, so the result is genuinely useful to you.
2. Ask the approved AI assistant (or draft the question mentally): "Find two public datasets about <topic> and give me the exact source URLs."
3. Open each suggested URL in your browser and check three things: does the page load, is it actually a dataset or metadata page, and is it current?
4. Discard anything that 404s, redirects to a generic homepage, or is not what was promised, that is a hallucinated or stale link, and naming it is part of the learning.
5. Post only the confirmed link in Zoom chat using the capture template: `VERIFIED: <url>` plus a few words on what the dataset is. In the room without chat? Add a Mural sticky in the same format on the board linked above.
6. Open one classmate's `VERIFIED:` post and check it yourself, does it pass your three checks too? Reply with what you found.

## Key takeaway 💡
An AI assistant is a discovery engine, not an authority, the only dataset link worth sharing is one you opened and checked yourself.

## Study further 📚
- [Data.gov](https://www.data.gov), the U.S. federal open-data catalog; the first place to check whether a suggested dataset really exists.
- [Open Government Portal, Government of Canada](https://open.canada.ca/en), Canada's official open-data and open-information search, a reliable verification target.
- [What are AI hallucinations?, IBM Think](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/ai-hallucinations), why language models invent convincing but fake links and citations.

## Common mistake to name ⚠️
Posting the AI's long, confident answer with no verified source, a list of three suggested links where zero were opened. Require the `VERIFIED:` template: one link, opened by a human, or it does not count.

## If finished early ⏩
Verify the second dataset as well, or tighten your prompt with a constraint, "only .gov or .gc.ca sources updated in the last two years", and note whether the suggestions improve.

## ⭐ Bonus (optional)
Repeat the hunt without AI: search data.gov or open.canada.ca directly for the same topic and compare the two routes. Which found the better dataset faster, and how much verification time did the AI route cost you? Post your verdict in one chat line.
