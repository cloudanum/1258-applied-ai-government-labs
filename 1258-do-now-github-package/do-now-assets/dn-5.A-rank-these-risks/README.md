# Do Now 5.A: Rank These Risks 🎯

**Time:** 5 minutes
**Format:** No special tooling or environment required, this activity is done mentally and captured directly on the Mural board (or in Zoom chat if you are remote).
**Mural:** https://app.mural.co/t/day10626/m/day10626/1790240799056/a4b3b37ad0e4cf67f7ff02483c02f633f8dfb96d

## Goal
Place eight AI risk stickies (R1–R8) on the 3×3 likelihood/impact grid for your own agency's context, and drag the two you would fund first into the FUND FIRST zone. You walk away with a ranked board, one written defense of a pick, and one respectful disagreement.

**Assets:** Risk stickies R1–R8 plus the 3×3 likelihood/impact grid (`risk-grid.png`).

**🏛️ Framework link:** [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework), The RMF's Govern, Map, Measure, Manage functions are the organizing logic behind ranking risks before paying to mitigate them.

## Run steps 🪜
1. Scan all eight risk stickies R1–R8: training data (R1, R8), inputs (R2, R3), the model or service itself (R4, R5, R6), or the people running it (R7).
2. Rate each one's likelihood and impact for YOUR agency, not the headlines, and drag it onto the 3×3 grid (see `risk-grid.png` for the axes).
3. Drag your top two risks into the FUND FIRST zone.
4. Comment on one FUND FIRST pick naming both likelihood and impact in one sentence, e.g. R3 prompt injection: "every submitted document reaches the prompt, so likelihood is high, and hijacked instructions make impact high."
5. Add one disagreement comment on a colleague's ranking, using the facilitator question on that sticky (for R1: "who controls training data and labels?").
6. Remote? Post `FUND FIRST: R3, R7, because ___` in Zoom chat and reply to one ranking you disagree with.

## Key takeaway 💡
Risk ranking is a funding decision, not a fear contest, the two risks you fund first should have the highest likelihood and impact in your own agency's context, and you should be able to defend each pick in one sentence.

## Study further 📚
- [Adversarial Machine Learning taxonomy (NIST AI 100-2e2023), NIST CSRC](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/ai/100/2/e2023/final), the federal taxonomy behind risks like poisoning, evasion, extraction, and inference.
- [OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications, OWASP](https://owasp.org/www-project-top-10-for-large-language-model-applications/), the community-ranked list of LLM risks, including prompt injection.
- [MITRE ATLAS, MITRE](https://atlas.mitre.org/), real-world tactics and case studies of attacks on AI-enabled systems.

## Common mistake to name ⚠️
Ranking by fear headlines instead of likelihood and impact in your own agency context, require every FUND FIRST pick to name both before it counts.

## If finished early ⏩
Choose your top risk and name the first metric that would tell you it is getting worse, for prompt injection (R3), for example, the share of submitted documents containing instruction-like text.
