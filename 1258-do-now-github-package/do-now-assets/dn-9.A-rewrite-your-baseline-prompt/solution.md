# Sample / solution

## Why this approach works
The rewrite works because it converts a wish into an instruction set. A baseline prompt leaves the model to guess four things: who it is, who will read the output, what it may use, and what the answer should look like, and every wrong guess becomes your cleanup work. Adding role, audience, source limits, output format, and a refusal rule removes the guessing, and the grounding or citation requirement is usually the single biggest change because it turns fluent invention into checkable quotation. Naming the one biggest change with its technique category, rather than just saying "the new one is better," is what makes the improvement repeatable: next time you know which lever to pull first. The weaker intuitive move, rewriting from memory with no saved P0, fails the baseline-first pattern, because a new prompt that feels better proves nothing. Keeping the prompt private and sharing only the category is not a limitation of the exercise, it is the discipline that lets you practice on a real work prompt.

## A complete solution
A realistic P0, the kind people actually write: "Summarize this email and tell me what to do."

A complete P10 rewrite: "You are a senior correspondence officer in a municipal permits office. Your reader is an intake clerk who is not a subject-matter expert and needs a quick brief before responding. Using only the email pasted below, summarize the resident's request in three bullet points, list any deadlines or fees it mentions, and end with one recommended next action. Quote the exact sentence from the email that supports each bullet, and if the email does not state something, write 'not stated' instead of guessing. Do not invent policy, cite regulations, or draft the reply itself."

The private one-line note, in the capture format: `biggest change: added explicit constraints + citation requirement (technique category: constraints).` The defense a learner should be able to give aloud: "The citation requirement changed the output most, because forcing the model to quote the email instead of paraphrasing freely made the two places where it had been inventing a deadline immediately visible." The bonus Bloom's check follows naturally: this P10 deliberately stays at L1/L2, retrieve and summarize, because the summary must be checkable against the source email. Climbing one level above the home level set in Do Now 0.2 is a separate prompt decision, not an automatic upgrade of this one.
